A little about me

Numbers tell a story when you ask the right questions.

I combine an accounting foundation with information systems and analytical thinking to help turn business questions into evidence-based decisions.

I hold a Diploma III in Accounting from Politeknik Negeri Semarang and am currently studying Information Systems at Universitas Dian Nuswantoro. My experience in finance administration and purchasing & receiving has taught me the importance of accurate records, reconciliation, and structured reporting.

I'm now building deeper data analytics skills through projects involving Python, pandas, SQL, data visualization, and interactive dashboards. I enjoy connecting the technical work—cleaning, validating, and analyzing data—with the business context behind it.

My approach: validate the data, make the insight understandable, and connect it to an action.
